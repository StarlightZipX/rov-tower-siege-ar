The Female, or Classic, Announcer pack is the base announcer for [[League of Legends]]. Besides the current playable PvP maps such as [[Summoner's Rift]] (5v5) and [[Howling Abyss]] ([[ARAM]]), The Female Classic Announcer has also been used on discontinued maps and modes such as [[Twisted Treeline]] (3v3), [[Snowdown Showdown]] ([[Legend of the Poro King]]), [[Crystal Scar]] ([[Dominion]]), and [[Nexus Siege]].
