The '''announcer''' in the game announces all events that is happening through out the game.

They are voiced by Cristina Vee (2016-present) and Aimee Smith (2020-present; Quick Chats only).

== Trivia ==
*Quadra Kill and Penta Kill were the old Maniac and Savage, while Ace was the old Wiped Out.
*Aside from voicing the announcer, Cristina Vee also voices the old version of {{hi|Kagura}} and {{hi|Lolita}}.
*Back in 2018, there were rumors that {{Aq|Announcer.kill11.ogg|Maniac!}} would be changed to {{Aq|Announcer.kill29.ogg|Frenzy Kill!}}, but there was no confirmation from the developers.
